Who's evil?
Lives: 3
Click players to mark good or evil, then press GUESS. Correct guesses get confirmed.
Green arrow: Claims target is good.
Red arrow: Claims target is evil.
Only the good players can be trusted. Your job is to identify who are good and who are evil.
How to Play
You know when you play social deduction games like mafia or blood on the clock tower. Then often there are good and evil players all claiming what teams other players are on. For example Alex might say "Blake is evil and Casey is good". The problem is that if Alex is evil you don't know if their claims are true. Since I'm a mathematician i just abstracted away the sentences into red and green arrows. Inspired by the Clues by Sam game (if it was cool and the evil people actually lied).
